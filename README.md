# CampusFix

## Smart Campus Complaint Management System

CampusFix is a web-based campus complaint management system designed for students and staff of Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University), Mullana.

The system aims to provide a centralized and organized platform where students can report campus-related problems instead of having to visit different offices or departments to communicate their complaints.

---

## 📌 About the Project

Students may face different problems around the university campus, such as:

- Electrical and lighting problems
- Water leakage or water supply issues
- Washroom and sanitation problems
- Cleanliness and garbage issues
- Broken desks, chairs and furniture
- Fan and AC problems
- Wi-Fi and internet issues
- Laboratory equipment problems
- Classroom infrastructure problems
- Hostel maintenance issues
- Parking and pathway problems
- Safety and security concerns
- Other campus maintenance problems

CampusFix is designed to bring these complaints into one organized system.

A student can register, log in and submit a complaint by providing relevant information such as the complaint category, location, priority, description and an optional image.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

The main purpose of CampusFix is to make campus problem reporting:

- Simple
- Organized
- Accessible
- Faster
- Transparent

The project aims to improve communication between students and the university administration and help campus-related problems reach the appropriate department.

---

## ⭐ Main Features

### Student Registration

Students can create an account by providing information such as:

- Full Name
- Student ID
- Department / Programme
- University Email
- Password

### Student Login

Registered students can use their Student ID or email and password to access the complaint system.

### Complaint Submission

Students can submit campus complaints by selecting:

- Complaint Category
- Location
- Priority
- Detailed Description
- Problem Image

### Complaint Categories

The system includes categories such as:

- Wi-Fi / Internet
- Electricity
- Water
- Hostel
- Classroom
- Laboratory
- Library
- Cafeteria
- Cleanliness
- Transport
- Other

### Location Selection

Students can identify where the problem occurred by selecting a campus location.

### Image Upload

Students can optionally upload an image to provide visual information about the reported problem.
