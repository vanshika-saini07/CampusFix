About CampusFix

Understanding the purpose and vision behind the system

About CampusFix

CampusFix is a digital complaint management system designed for the students and administration of Maharishi Markandeshwar (Deemed to be University).

The purpose of CampusFix is to provide a centralized platform where students can report problems they experience across the university campus and communicate those concerns in an organized manner.

Instead of depending completely on manual reporting, visiting different offices or trying to identify the appropriate department, students can use one platform to submit and monitor their complaints.

The Problem We Aim to Address

Students may encounter a variety of problems during their daily activities on campus. These can include maintenance issues, electrical faults, water problems, cleanliness concerns, classroom facilities, hostel-related issues, internet connectivity and other infrastructure problems.

When complaints are reported through different channels, it can become difficult for students to know where to report an issue or how to follow up on it. This can also make complaint management less organized for the concerned departments.

CampusFix aims to provide a single and structured reporting process for these campus-related concerns.

Our Solution

CampusFix brings the complaint reporting process into one digital platform. A student can log in, provide information about a problem, select its category and location, and submit the complaint.

Supporting information such as an image can also help communicate the problem more clearly to the concerned department.

Once a complaint is submitted, its progress can be tracked through the system. This creates a more organized connection between the student reporting the issue and the department responsible for addressing it.

Who Can Use CampusFix?

  • Students — Report campus-related problems and monitor the progress of their complaints.
  • Administration — Receive and manage complaints submitted by students.
  • Concerned Departments — Review assigned problems and take appropriate action.

Types of Problems That Can Be Reported

  • Electrical and lighting problems
  • Water supply and leakage
  • Washroom and sanitation issues
  • Cleanliness and waste management
  • Classroom infrastructure and furniture
  • AC and fan problems
  • Wi-Fi and internet connectivity
  • Computer and laboratory equipment
  • Hostel maintenance
  • Parking and campus pathways
  • Garden and outdoor maintenance
  • Safety and security concerns
  • Other campus-related problems

Key Features of CampusFix

  • Student Registration and Login — Provides students with an individual account for using the complaint system.
  • Complaint Submission — Allows students to provide details about a campus problem.
  • Problem Categorization — Helps organize complaints according to the type of issue.
  • Location Details — Allows the student to specify where the problem occurred.
  • Image Upload — Provides supporting visual evidence when required.
  • Status Tracking — Allows students to follow the progress of their complaints.
  • Centralized Management — Helps organize complaints so that they can be reviewed by the appropriate administration or department.

How the CampusFix System Works

Step 1 — Student Registration
A new student creates an account on the platform.

Step 2 — Student Login
The student accesses their account.

Step 3 — Complaint Submission
The student enters the problem details, category, location and supporting information.

Step 4 — Complaint Review
The complaint can be reviewed and directed towards the appropriate department.

Step 5 — Problem Resolution
The concerned department works towards resolving the issue.

Step 6 — Status Tracking
The student can check the current status of the complaint.

Benefits

  • Reduces confusion about where to report a problem.
  • Creates a centralized record of reported complaints.
  • Makes important complaint information easier to organize.
  • Provides students with better visibility of complaint progress.
  • Helps departments identify recurring campus problems.
  • Encourages students to actively contribute to campus improvement.

Our Vision

CampusFix aims to support a more responsive and organized campus environment where students can raise genuine concerns easily and the concerned authorities can manage those concerns systematically.

The long-term vision is to develop CampusFix into a comprehensive digital platform that improves communication, transparency and campus maintenance.

Future Scope

The system can be extended in the future with features such as notifications, complaint priority levels, department dashboards, analytics, complaint history, automated categorization and administrative reporting.
