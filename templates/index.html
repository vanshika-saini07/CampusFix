Welcome to CampusFix

CampusFix provides students with a structured way to report campus-related problems to the concerned administration or department.

From electrical faults and water problems to cleanliness, classroom, hostel and internet issues, CampusFix provides one centralized platform for reporting and tracking complaints.

CampusFix Features

Complaint Reporting

Submit campus-related problems through an organized online complaint process.

Status Tracking

Follow the progress of submitted complaints and stay informed about their status.

Evidence Upload

Provide images and other information to clearly describe the reported problem.
